22:T470,## Application guidance

Use member-level series to estimate internal variability while keeping results grouped by parent GCM. Compare balanced parent-model summaries with any pooled-member result.

## Limits and evaluation checks

The ensemble samples only three CMIP6 models under one future scenario. The paper identifies all three as relatively high climate-sensitivity models [Hartke et al. (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-04205-z), so also examine how their forced responses compare with a broader model ensemble. Do not interpret 200 members as 200 independent climate models or as complete scenario uncertainty. EC-Earth3 starts later, which affects common-period comparisons.

## Documentation and expert contributions

The linked metadata sources describe this archive. Related publication: [Hartke et al. (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-04205-z).
